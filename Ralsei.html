Description

Ralsei is a goat-like Darkner prince and one of the main protagonists of Deltarune, serving as the prophesied "Prince from the Dark" and the party's dedicated healer and support character. He is characterized by his gentle, humble, and pacifist nature, strongly advocating for non-violent solutions and healing magic over combat.
