Description

Kris Dreemurr is the androgynous, introverted human protagonist of Deltarune, adopted by the monster Dreemurr family. They are the only human living in the monster-populated town of Hometown, which contributes to their reputation as a quiet, somewhat eerie loner who struggles with social connections. When entering a Dark World, Kris transforms into a knight-like figure with cyan skin, navy hair, and silver armor, donning a pink cape. In this role, they serve as the party leader, specializing in ACTing and using a sword that can transform into a shield.
