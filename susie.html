Description

Susie is a reptilian monster and one of the main protagonists of Deltarune, serving as the second hero of the prophecy. She is a tall, teenage student in Hometown with lavender-purple skin, a snout, sharp yellow teeth, and long, messy aubergine hair. Her Light World attire consists of a long purple jacket, a white shirt, and torn jeans, while her Dark World outfit features a sleeveless purple top, dark plum trousers, and spiked bracelets. Initially portrayed as a rude, sarcastic, and violent bully who threatens physical harm and struggles academically, Susie possesses a facade of aloofness stemming from low self-esteem, a history of moving homes, and a lack of genuine friends. Despite her aggressive exterior and habit of eating non-food items like chalk and pinecones, she is deeply empathetic, loyal, and protective of those who earn her trust.
