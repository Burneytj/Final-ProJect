Description

Noelle Holiday is a mid-teens anthropomorphic reindeer monster and a Lightner from Hometown, serving as one of Kris’s classmates, neighbors, and childhood friends. She is the youngest daughter of Carol Holiday, the Mayor of Hometown, and Rudy Holiday, who is hospitalized with an unnamed illness. Noelle lives in a mansion on the northwest side of town and is noted for being the smartest student in her class, though she is also timid, shy, and easily flustered.
