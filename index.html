Story(So Far)

The latest chapter in Deltarune right now is Chapter 5, which is titled "Festival Day". It's Festival time. But things take a twisted turn when a Dark Fountain appears in Asgore Dreemurr's shop Flower King . This leads the heroes to cut the Festival with Noelle short and jump into this Japan-inspired Dark World, which is filled with beautiful plants, shiny grass fields, wacky Yokai-like creatures, and even a glorious Japanese castle. Along the way we also meet Flowery, Asgore's right-hand man while also meeting his best 6 flower buddies Orange, Green, Yellow, Blue, Aqua, and Seth. These 7 flowers try to stop Kris, Susie, and Ralsei from closing their Dark Fountain while also becoming friends with them along the way.

About The Creator

Robert F. "Toby" Fox (born October 11, 1991, in Manchester, New Hampshire) is an American video game developer and composer best known for creating the critically acclaimed role-playing games Undertale (2015) and Deltarune (2018–present). He is a self-taught musician who initially gained recognition for composing music for the webcomic Homestuck and creating the EarthBound ROM hack Halloween Hack under the alias Radiation. Toby and his artistic best friend Temmie Chang (who is also a game creator herself) are responsible for the beautiful, lore-packed, theory-crazed, and absolutely freedom filled world that we know as Deltarune.
