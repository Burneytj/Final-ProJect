Story(So Far)

The latest chapter in Deltarune right now is Chapter 5, which is titled "Festival Day". It's now time for the carnival-inspired Festival, which everyone is excited for (besides Kris). But things take a twisted turn when a Dark Fountain appears in Asgore Dreemurr's shop Flower King . This leads Kris and Susie to cut the Festival with Noelle short and jump into this Japan-inspired Dark World, which is filled with beautiful plants, shiny grass fields, wacky Yokai-like creatures, and even a glorious Japanese castle. Along the way we also meet Flowery, Asgore's right-hand man while also meeting his best 6 flower buddies Orange, Green, Yellow, Blue, Aqua, and Seth. These 7 flowers try to stop Kris, Susie, and Ralsei from closing their Dark Fountain, putting them through puzzles and platform-styled obstacles using their plant magic, while also becoming friends with each flower along the way, ultimately reaching a climax where the Fun Gang faces against the 6 flowers and Flowery at their full Omega strength. This chapter concludes with Flowery getting Swooned by the Roaring Knight after they make a second Dark Fountain, putting him in critical condition after their fight, leaving it to Kris and Susie to try saving him in the Light World after closing the second Dark Fountain. Susie and Kris go home after finding the second code for the Bunker while speculating about Noelle leaving the festival in a hurry to check on her father Rudy Holiday, being in the hospital after his sickness gets him into an accident while helping with the festival. The Weird Route is not written in this description of the current story.

About The Creator

Robert F. "Toby" Fox (born October 11, 1991, in Manchester, New Hampshire) is an American video game developer and composer best known for creating the critically acclaimed role-playing games Undertale (2015) and Deltarune (2018–present). He is a self-taught musician who initially gained recognition for composing music for the webcomic Homestuck and creating the EarthBound ROM hack Halloween Hack under the alias Radiation. Toby and his artistic best friend Temmie Chang (who is also a game creator herself) are responsible for the beautiful, lore-packed, theory-crazed, and absolutely freedom filled world that we know as Deltarune.
